FILM SELECTION

SELECTING THE FILM FORMAT

The scanner can accomodate 35 mm and APS film. The optional APS adapter AD-10 is required to scan APS film.

<How to select film format>

  1. In the top left corner of the utility window, click the arrow next to the film-format drop-down list to display the formats.
  2. Select the format of the film to be scanned.
    • The list box always displays the selected film format.

SELECTING THE FILM TYPE

All types of 35mm and APS film can be scanned. Always select the appropriate film type before scanning. Positive film, or slides, has a normal appearance when viewed. Negative film will appear unusual because the tones and colors are reversed. Color negative film also has a strong orange cast caused by its integral color mask.

<How to select film type>

  1. In the top left corner of the utility window, click the arrow next to the film-type drop-down list to display the film options.
  2. Select the type of film to be scanned.
    • The options differ according to the film format selected.
