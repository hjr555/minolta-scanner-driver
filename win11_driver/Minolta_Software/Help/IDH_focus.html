FOCUSING THE SCANNER

AUTOFOCUS AT SCAN

The DiMAGE Scan Dual III can automatically focus on the film image during the prescan and final scan. The autofocus function is activated in the preferences dialog box. Simply click the autofocus-at-scan check box. Scanning time will increase when the autofocus system is used.

* The autofocus-at-scan function focuses on the center of the image. If the center of the image has no contrast or detail, the autofocus system may not be able to focus: use the point AF or manual focus functions (see below).

POINT AF

If the film is warped or curled, or if the center of the image has no contrast or detail, the point AF function can be used to focus the scanner on any point within the prescan image area. For best results, select an area on or near the subject of the image that has detail or contrast.

<How to use>

  1. Make a prescan of the image to be focused.

  2. Click the point AF button.
    • The shape of the mouse cursor will change.
  3. Click on the point of the image to be used for focus.
    • The focus will be adjusted automatically and the newly focused prescan will be displayed.

MANUAL FOCUS

Manual control over the autofocus system is possible.

<How to use>

  1. Make a prescan of the image to be focused.

  2. Click the manual focus button.
    • The shape of the mouse cursor will change.
  3. Click on the point of the image to be used for focus.
    • The manual-focus dialog box will be displayed.
  4. Adjust the slider until the black and white bars are at their longest extension for sharpest focus.
    • The black bar indicates the change in focus. The white bar indicates the longest extent of the black bar. When both bars reach their longest extent, the film image is in focus.
  5. Click the OK button to set the focus.
    • The scanner will adjust the focus and the newly focused prescan will be displayed.
