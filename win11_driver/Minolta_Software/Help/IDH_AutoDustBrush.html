AUTO DUST BRUSH

The Auto Dust Brush function uses software processing to reduce the visibility of dust on the image surface.

<Procedure>

  1. Press the Auto Dust Brush button in the main window.
    • The Auto Dust Brush tab becomes active.
  2. Click the Auto Dust Brush tab to open the tab.
  3. Set the dust removal level.
    • A simulated image is displayed that depicts the dust-reduced image surface after applying the specified level of dust removal.

APPLYING A DUST REMOVAL LEVEL TO ALL FRAMES

Pressing on the Apply all frames button applies the current dust removal level to all frames.
The image preview for other frames for which the dust removal level is applied are erased.

RESETTING DUST REMOVAL LEVEL

Pressing the Reset button resets the dust removal level to the middle value.
To reset all frames, press the Reset button and then press the Apply all frames button.

FULL-SCREEN VIEW

Pressing the Full-screen view button displays the entire corrected image to which the Auto Dust Brush tab is applied.
