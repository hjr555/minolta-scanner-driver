SAVING AND LOADING IMAGE-CORRECTION JOBS

IMAGE-CORRECTION JOB

The settings used to correct an image can be saved and applied to other images. This feature can save time by allowing image-correction settings to be applied to certain film types or shooting conditions.

SAVING AN IMAGE-CORRECTION JOB

<How to save>

  1. After making corrections to the prescan image, click the save image-correction Job button in the image-correction tab.
    • The image-correction Job registry window will be displayed.
  2. Input the Job name and click the OK button.
    • The current image-correction settings are saved as an image-correction Job under the specified name.

LOADING AN IMAGE-CORRECTION JOB

A saved image-correction Job can be applied to the displayed image.

<How to load>

  1. Click the load image-correction Job button in the image correction tab.
    • The image-correction Job selection window will be displayed.
  2. Select the image-correction Job and click the OK button to apply it to the displayed prescan image.
    • Multiple image-correction Jobs can be loaded. Each Job is displayed in the snapshot display area. Simply click on the Job thumbnail to preview the effect on the prescan image.
