TROUBLESHOOTING

Q: The scanned image has a severe color-balance problem. What can be done?

A: Use Manual Set-up.

Remove the holder, then close the door on the front of the scanner.

<How To - Windows>

  1. With the driver software active, press the Ctrl, Shift, and I keys simultaneously.

<How To - Macintosh>

  1. With the driver software active, press the Command, Shift, and I keys simultaneously.
