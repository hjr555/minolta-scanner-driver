THE PRESCAN

Unlike an index scan, a prescan can have image processing applied to it before the final scan is made. This allows the image information to be optimized at the scan.

PRESCANNING

<How to make a prescan>

  1. Select the frame or frames to be prescanned.
  2. Click the prescan button.
    • Double clicking on the index scan frame will also start a prescan of that image.

CROPPING PRESCAN IMAGE

The more precise prescan image can be displayed by prescanning the crop area again.

<How To>

  1. Click on the Cropping Prescan button.

AUTO CROPPING

Auto cropping eliminates blank space around the image area.

<Procedure>

  1. Click the auto-cropping button.
    • Each time the auto-cropping button is pressed, the cropping frame cycles through the following order: crop to outside edge > crop to inside edge > entire window. Click the crop-prescan button to view the results; another prescan of the cropped area will be made.
  2. Click the crop-prescan button to view the results; another prescan of the cropped area will be made.

MANUALLY CROPPING THE PRESCAN IMAGE

Cropping is a method of recomposing the image by eliminating unnecessary space around the subject. Many images are improved by removing extraneous elements in the background.

<How to manually adjust the cropping area>

  1. Click the auto-cropping button.
  2. Place the mouse pointer on the cropping frame and click and drag to resize or move the marquee.
    • To resize the cropping frame, click on the edge of the frame and drag.
    • Click and drag outside the marquee to define a new cropping frame.
    • To move the cropping frame without changing its size, place the pointer inside the frame and click and drag it to a new position.
    • Click the crop-prescan button to view the results; another prescan of the cropped area will be made.

ROTATE THE PRESCAN IMAGE

The image can be rotated to display it correctly. The final scan will reflect the change in orientation.

<How to rotate the prescan image>

  1. Click the rotate-right or rotate-left button in the prescan tab.
    • The image will be rotated in 90 degree increments either clockwise (right) or counterclockwise (left).

FLIP THE PRESCAN IMAGE

The image can be flipped along the horizontal axis (left-to-right) or the vertical axis (top-to-bottom). This will create a mirror image of the prescan.

<How to flip the prescan>

  1. Click the flip-vertical or flip-horizontal button to reverse the prescan image.

FIT-TO-WINDOW BUTTON

To view the entire image area in the utility window, click the fit-to-window button. If the window is resized, the prescan image will be automatically enlarged or reduced to fit the window.

MAGNIFYING OR REDUCING THE PRESCAN IMAGE

The prescan image can be enlarged for closer examination. If the fit-to-window function is active, the image cannot be magnified or reduced.

<How to use the zoom tool>

  1. Click on the zoom button in the prescan tab.
    • The pointer will change to a magnifying glass with a plus sign (+) in the center.
  2. Click on the area of the image to be enlarged. The clicked position will be the center of the magnified view.
    • When the maximum magnification is reached, the plus sign (+) will disappear.
  3. Press and hold the Ctrl key (Windows) or the option key (Macintosh), the magnifying glass will be displayed with a minus sign (-) in the center. Click on the image to zoom out.

GRAB BUTTON

The grab tool can be used to scroll a prescan image. Click the grab button to activate the tool. Simply click and drag the image to view the image area. The grab button cannot be used when the fit-to-window function is active

AE LOCK

The AE lock function sets the scanner exposure based on the exposure of a specific prescan. This exposure can be applied to scans of other images. This is useful when scanning high or low key images; a reference image with a normal tonal range can be used to lock the exposure. Also, when scanning a bracketed series of exposures, locking the AE system will show the exposure difference in the series. The AE lock is disabled when scanning slides. If the slides are badly exposed, the AE lock function can be activated by checking the auto-expose-for-slides option in the preferences dialog box.

<How to lock the scanner exposure>

  1. Make a prescan of the reference image.
  2. Click the AE lock button to set the scanner exposure.

AE AREA SELECTION

AE area selection allows a small area within the image to be used to determine the scanner exposure. This function can be used with high or low key images. To use AE area selection with slides, check the auto-expose-for-slides option in the preferences dialog box.

<How to use AE area selection>

  1. Press the shift key to display the AE area.
    • The AE area in indicated with a solid frame.
  2. While pressing the shift key, change the AE area.
    • To resize the AE area, click on the edge of the frame and drag.
    • Click and drag outside the frame to define a new AE area.
    • To move the AE area without changing its size, place the pointer inside the frame and click and drag it to a new position.
  3. Place the AE area over the section of the image to be used to determine the exposure.
    • Usually placing the area over the subject of the picture will produce exellent results. The area should represent on average the mid-tone of the image.
  4. Click the prescan button to view the effect on the exposure.

CROPPING APS IMAGES

When APS film is scanned, the CHP button is displayed in the prescan window. Clicking the CHP button cycles the cropping frame through the C, H, and P APS framing formats.

<How to crop APS images>

  1. Click the CHP button until the desired frame is displayed.
    • To resize the cropping frame, click on the edge of the frame and drag.
    • Click and drag outside the marquee to define a new cropping frame.
    • To move the cropping frame without changing its size, place the pointer inside the frame and click and drag it to a new position.
    • Click the crop-prescan button to view the results; another prescan of the cropped area will be made.

FRAME NUMBER DISPLAY

The frame number of the displayed prescan image and the total number of images in the film holder are displayed at the top of the prescan window. Clicking the arrow buttons on each side of the frame number will display the previous or next frame. If those images have not been prescanned, a prescan will automatically start.

RGB DISPLAY

Placing the mouse pointer on the prescan image will display the RGB information of that point. The information is displayed at the top of the window. The numbers indicate the brightness level from 0 to 255. The first number is the value for the original prescan and the second number indicates any image correction changes. Simply press and hold the shift key (Windows) or command key (Macintosh) to display the corresponding CMY levels.
